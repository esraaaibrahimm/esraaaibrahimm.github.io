Free Spirit Tours: ranking tour packages for Arab travellers

Securing top rankings for commercial travel keywords on a large Arabic, English and Russian tourism website.

Client site
freespirit.tours
Industry
Tourism
Market
Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Jordan, Kuwait, UAE

2021 to 2024

Commercial-keyword content plan, tour-package pages, link building
  • 281KClicks16 months, Search Console

  • 2.5%Click-through rate

Challenge

Heavy competition in the tourism sector, and a need to focus on high-conversion commercial keywords across the Saudi, Egyptian, Jordanian, Kuwaiti and Emirati markets.

Approach

I implemented a content plan centred on commercial keywords, focusing on tour packages. I also worked on off-page efforts, building quality backlinks to strengthen the website’s authority.

Results

Over 16 months Search Console shows 281K clicks and 11.4M impressions at a 2.5% click-through rate and an average position of 7.8. The site ranks on the first page for its most important keywords and drives significant organic traffic from high-intent users. In November 2026 Google’s AI Overview still cites it for tour programs in Georgia.
