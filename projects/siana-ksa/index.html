siana-ksa.com: localised SEO for an appliance-repair business in Saudi Arabia

Doubling traffic for an appliance-repair business with a localisation-led strategy that sets it apart from competitors.

Challenge

Appliance-repair searches in Saudi cities are crowded with near-identical service sites, so ranking alone was not enough. The site needed to stand out from competitors.

Approach

I built a localisation-led strategy around city- and service-specific content, differentiated from what competitors publish.

Results

Search Console shows 20.3K clicks and 2.06M impressions at an average position of 6.9, with daily clicks climbing from roughly 100 to roughly 200 across the period. Google Analytics 4 recorded 39K active users between 1 August 2025 and 30 June 2026. Google’s AI Overview features the business for washing-machine repair in Taif and water-cooler repair in Jeddah. The reported conversion rate reached 17%.
