U-Trade Furniture: technical SEO and AI visibility for a US furniture retailer

Overcoming technical SEO challenges and growing keyword visibility for an online furniture retailer in a fiercely competitive market.

Challenge

The website had significant technical issues, including numerous page redirects and errors that hampered performance. Competition in the US furniture market made the task harder.

Approach

I tackled the technical problems first, resolving redirects and improving the site structure. At the same time I implemented a well-rounded content plan targeting relevant furniture keywords, and started off-page work focused on local listings and backlink growth.

Results

In three months the keyword count rose from 944 to 3,600, growth of over 280%. Daily impressions increased by 660% and daily clicks by 169%. Over time, sessions grew 900%+ through SEO alone, with no social media or paid ads, and the store consistently hit its $12,000 monthly sales target during US seasonal peaks. Sales have also come directly from ChatGPT, and the store is the “best match” in ChatGPT answers for Homey Design sets in California.
