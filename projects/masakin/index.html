Masakin: Arabic real-estate SEO in Saudi Arabia

Driving traffic and sales by optimising commercial keywords and improving visibility for real-estate searches in the Saudi market.

Client site
masakin.net
Industry
Real estate
Market
Saudi Arabia
Period
2022 to 2024

Arabic commercial keywords, content plan, off-page SEO, local listings
  • 15.1KClicks16 months, Search Console
  • 657KImpressions16 months
  • 2.3%Click-through rate

Challenge

The website needed to rank for competitive real-estate keywords in Arabic, such as apartments and villas, while improving user engagement and conversions.

Approach

I focused on commercial keywords built around the Arabic words for villa and apartment, with a detailed content plan: categorising the types of real estate and services offered, adding new projects, and building blog content. I also worked on off-page SEO and local listings aimed at the Saudi market.

Results

Over 16 months Search Console shows 15.1K clicks and 657K impressions at a 2.3% click-through rate. The website ranks on the first page for key real-estate terms and achieved significant traffic growth, which directly contributed to increased real-estate sales through the platform. Google’s AI Overview still cites the site for land for sale in Jeddah with installment plans.
