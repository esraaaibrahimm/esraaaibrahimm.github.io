Improving rankings for competitive commercial keywords and driving organic traffic from high-intent users in Egypt and Saudi Arabia.

Challenge

The digital marketing industry is highly competitive, with many companies targeting the same commercial keywords. The challenge was to stand out in a crowded space.

Approach

I developed a targeted content strategy centred on high-value commercial keywords, optimising service pages, blogs and landing pages to match search intent. On the off-page side I built a strong backlink profile through guest posts and partnerships with authoritative websites in the industry.

Results

Over 16 months Search Console shows 29.6K clicks and 1.74M impressions, with a steady upward trend in both. The website ranks on the first page for critical commercial keywords, with a significant increase in organic clicks from the target regions. In 2026 Google’s AI Overview lists Digitallity among the top web-design companies in Egypt.
