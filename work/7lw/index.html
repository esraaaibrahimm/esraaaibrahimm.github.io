Challenge

The GCC financial niche is highly saturated. The website needed structural and regional-targeting improvements to compete and stand out across different Gulf markets.

Approach

I ran a layout and user-behaviour audit to improve the site structure. I then deployed a localised content plan targeting high-converting, long-tail financial keywords while refining the site’s regional visibility.

Results

Comparing 22 October 2025 to 21 June 2026 with the period before it, Search Console shows clicks up from 4.04K to 7.85K, impressions up from 138K to 672K, and average position improved from 12.3 to 8. Click-through rate moved from 2.9% to 1.2% as the site started appearing for far more queries. Google’s AI features showed the site 48.2K times in six months. Conversion rate reached 4%.
