Challenge

The shop sells ultra-premium, high-ticket culinary knives, so the audience is exceptionally narrow. The task was to reach high-intent affluent buyers rather than generic shoppers in a highly competitive market.

Approach

I optimised premium category layouts and refined a localised content plan around high-end culinary craftsmanship. To avoid generic competition I deployed targeted keyword frameworks and tracked user behaviour closely to lift conversion.

Results

Search Console shows 1.47K clicks in the last six months against 348 in the six months before, and 56.2K impressions against 8.31K. Average position held at 7.9 (previously 8.1) while volume grew, and click-through rate moved from 4.2% to 2.6%. Conversion rate rose above 1.5%. Google’s AI Overview and ChatGPT both name the brand when asked where to buy Damascus knives in the UAE.
